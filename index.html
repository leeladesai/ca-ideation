Problem statements

Six problems I think a filing month creates

These come from vendor research and public discussion. None has been confirmed by Chennai firms yet, which is why I am asking.

  • Chasing clients for documentsEvidence: medium-strongJuniors message clients again and again for bills and bank statements; the partner phones the late ones near the deadline.
  • Supplier invoices missing in GSTR-2BEvidence: mediumThe purchase register does not match what suppliers filed, so the client's input tax credit is at risk.
  • Tax notices found lateEvidence: strongGST and income-tax notices arrive by portal, email or WhatsApp and sit unseen until the reply window is nearly gone.
  • Notice replies written from scratchEvidence: mediumEach reply is drafted fresh, even when the firm has answered the same kind of notice many times before.
  • Clients calling for statusEvidence: mediumClients phone to ask whether their return is filed, and the calls land on the partner.
  • Collecting the firm's own feesEvidence: weakFees are paid late and reminders feel awkward to send to a long-standing client.
The key problem

Two candidates to solve first. You help pick.

Either one would be built so the agent finishes the job, not just sends a reminder. The other follows once the first works.

Candidate A

Document chasing

The agent knows what each client still owes this month, follows up on WhatsApp, then makes a polite Tamil call, and pushes what arrives into Tally.

Why first
Happens every cycle, for every client. The smallest thing a firm would pay for.
Watch out
Zoho Practice, Suvit and Jamku already collect documents.
Candidate B

Tax notices

Every notice is logged with its deadline the day it arrives, and a first draft reply is built from the firm's own past replies for the CA to approve.

Why first
Highest stakes and the strongest public evidence of pain.
Watch out
Several AI reply tools launched in 2025-26; a wrong draft carries liability.

Question 3 below asks which one you would want first. The design partners' answers decide it.

Design partnership

Build it with me, around how your office already works

What you give

  • A 30-minute conversation about your last filing month
  • 20 minutes every two weeks during the pilot to review what the agent did
  • Honest feedback, including "this is useless"

What you get

  • A place in shaping the system: what gets built, and in what order
  • First access to the agent, free, set up and run with you during the pilot
  • The anonymised findings from all firms

What I need to design it

  • How you ask clients for documents today: your usual WhatsApp message
  • Your monthly checklist for a typical GST client
  • 3 to 5 past notice replies, with client details removed
  • For the pilot: 30 to 50 client contacts and a Tally export

Your data stays in India, is used only for your firm, and is deleted when you ask or when the pilot ends. Nothing about money, penalties or legal positions goes to a client without your approval.
